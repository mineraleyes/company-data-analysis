# Stage 1 — The numbers

*1,079 mining companies. Size and trading as at 30 June 2026; commodity and property as at 31 July 2026.*

## Headline

**TSX holds 17% of the issuers and 92.8% of the market cap. These are two different populations sharing a sector label.**

| | TSX | TSXV |
|---|---|---|
| Issuers | 183 | 896 |
| Share of own board | 8% | 60% |
| Total market cap | C$1,053.6B | C$82.0B |
| Median market cap | C$1,080.3M | C$18.7M |
| Value traded H1 2026 | C$427.0B | C$19.9B |

Mining is a minor sector on the TSX and the **dominant** one on the TSXV.

## Concentration

**Half the TSX mining sector is ten companies. The TSXV is genuinely flat by comparison.**

- TSX top 10 = **54.8%** of TSX mining market cap
- Agnico Eagle alone = **10.6%**
- TSXV top 10 = **22.8%**

Only two TSXV miners exceed C$2B (Artemis Gold, Sigma Lithium).

## Size distribution

**57% of the TSXV mining board is below C$25M and collectively worth C$4.2B — a rounding error against the TSX's C$984B in the top band.**

| Band | TSX | TSXV |
|---|---|---|
| < C$5M | 2 | 217 |
| C$5–25M | 9 | 290 |
| C$25–100M | 15 | 215 |
| C$100–500M | 40 | 143 |

**The boards overlap:** 26 TSX names sit below C$100M and 174 TSXV names sit above it. Board is not a clean size proxy.

Distribution spans six orders of magnitude — log scale is mandatory for any chart.

## Commodity

**This is a gold sector with a copper problem attached. 658 of 1,079 companies have gold exposure — 61% of the entire universe.**

Exposure counts overlap, because most companies hold several commodities.

| Commodity | Companies | TSX | TSXV | Market cap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gold | 658 | 118 | 540 | C$892.7B |
| Copper | 353 | 62 | 291 | C$592.7B |
| Silver | 223 | 52 | 171 | C$457.8B |
| Lithium | 104 | 7 | 97 | C$16.5B |
| Nickel | 86 | 11 | 75 | C$137.1B |
| Zinc | 86 | 19 | 67 | C$163.5B |
| Uranium | 50 | 14 | 36 | C$95.4B |
| Platinum/PGM | 39 | 8 | 31 | C$76.7B |
| Rare Earths | 35 | 3 | 32 | C$4.0B |

**Most companies are polymetallic** — 462 hold more than one commodity, 525 hold exactly one, 92 disclose none. There are 132 distinct commodity sets, but they're concentrated: Gold alone (286), Gold+Copper (113) and Gold+Silver (74) cover 44% of the universe.

**Two commodities are almost entirely a TSXV story.** Lithium is 97 TSXV against 7 TSX; rare earths 32 against 3. These are the speculative frontier — huge company counts, tiny market caps. Lithium's 104 companies are worth C$16.5B combined, less than a fifth of uranium's 50 companies at C$95.4B.

**35 royalty and streaming companies hold C$161.9B — 14% of sector market cap.** They own no mines. Any per-company comparison that treats them as operators is distorted.

## Geography — where the assets are

**Only 513 of 1,079 companies hold Canadian property, against 975 headquartered in Canada. Vancouver is a financing hub, not a mining district.**

| Asset region | Companies | Market cap |

| Canada | 513 | C$612.6B |

**Of the 650 BC-headquartered companies, only 314 (48%) hold any Canadian property.** 159 hold Latin American assets, 150 US, 38 African. Ontario is the same story — 102 of 201.

**Sector-wide, 514 of 1,079 companies (48%) hold no assets in the region they're based in.**

Top asset jurisdictions: BC (168), ON (149), QC (125), Nevada (111), Mexico (88), Peru (53), Yukon (53), Saskatchewan (47), Argentina (46), Chile (41).

Most companies are single-jurisdiction — 668 of them. Versamet Royalties is the widest at 15, Barrick at 11.

## Liquidity

**The median TSX miner turns over twice as much of its market cap as the median TSXV miner — a real gap, but far smaller than the size gap.**

Turnover = H1 2026 value traded ÷ market cap.

| Percentile | TSX | TSXV |
|---|---|---|
| p25 | 0.117 | 0.068 |
| **p50** | **0.319** | **0.140** |
| p75 | 0.486 | 0.256 |

Small average trade sizes on both boards suggest a retail-dominated tape.

## The disclosure floor

**51 companies disclose neither a commodity nor a property.** Median market cap C$7.4M — but median turnover 0.128, so they are trading actively. Listed as miners, with nothing on record about what or where they mine.

## Caveats

- **The two source files are a month apart.** Commodity and property are 31 July; everything financial is 30 June.
- **224 TSXV miners (25%) have no listing date** — cohort cuts run on 672, not 896
- **467 TSXV miners (52%) have Other or missing listing type**
- **92 companies have no commodity disclosed**, 70 no property
- **19 TSXV nulls in trading data** — not zeros
- **10 companies** in the June issuer file had left by the July mining file
